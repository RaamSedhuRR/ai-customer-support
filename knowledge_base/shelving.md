# Shelving

## Models and finishes

Four flat-pack models, each in four finishes: natural oak, white, black, and walnut.

- HP-S3: 3-shelf bookcase, 106 cm high.
- HP-S5: 5-shelf bookcase, 176 cm high.
- HP-C4: cube unit 2x2, 77 cm high.
- HP-C9: cube unit 3x3, 112 cm high.

The model code is on the assembly manual and the box label.

## Parts lists

Part number = model code + panel number from the sticker on the panel. Example: HP-S3-04.

- HP-S3: 1 left side, 2 right side, 3 top, 4 shelf, 5 bottom, 6 back panel.
- HP-S5: 1 left side, 2 right side, 3 top, 4 to 6 shelves, 7 bottom, 8 back panel, 9 kick plate.
- HP-C4: 1 top, 2 bottom, 3 left side, 4 right side, 5 center divider, 6 shelf, 7 back panel.
- HP-C9: 1 top, 2 bottom, 3 left side, 4 right side, 5 and 6 dividers, 7 and 8 shelves, 9 back panel.

Hardware kits: HK-S for S models, HK-C for C models. Each kit holds all cam locks, dowels, screws, and the wall anchor.

## Assembly tips

- Lay the panels on the box or a rug to avoid scratches.
- Cam locks: the arrow points at the cam bolt hole. Turn clockwise about half a turn, until it stops.
- The back panel goes in with the finished side facing into the shelf.
- Tall units (HP-S5, HP-C9) must be fixed to the wall with the included anchor.
- Assembly takes 30 to 60 minutes for one person. A cordless screwdriver on a low setting is fine.

## Problems and what happens next

A colleague handles every shipment. Never promise one in a reply.

- Missing screws or hardware: ship the hardware kit for the model.
- 1 or 2 damaged panels: ship those panels by part number.
- 3 or more damaged panels, or a damaged frame: send a full replacement.
- Wrong finish delivered: exchange for the right finish.

To arrange anything, collect the order number, model, and damaged part numbers. A photo helps.
