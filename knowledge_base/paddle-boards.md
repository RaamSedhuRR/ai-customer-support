# Paddle boards

## Sizes

We sell three inflatable boards. Choose by rider weight, including gear.

- 10'6" All-round: riders up to 85 kg. For beginners and calm water.
- 11' All-round: riders up to 110 kg. The most stable board, also good for two light riders.
- 12'6" Touring: riders up to 130 kg. Faster and longer, for trips and experienced paddlers.

A rider between two sizes takes the larger board.

## What is in the box

Board, 3-piece adjustable paddle, double-action hand pump with gauge, removable center fin, coiled leash, valve wrench, and carry bag.

Part numbers:

- Fin: PB-FIN
- Pump: PB-PUMP
- Paddle: PB-PADDLE
- Leash: PB-LEASH
- Valve wrench: PB-WRENCH
- Carry bag: PB-BAG

## Inflation

Inflate to 12 to 15 PSI. Below 12 PSI the board bends in the middle.

Losing 1 to 2 PSI overnight when the air cools is normal and is not a leak.

## Valve check for slow leaks

Most slow leaks come from a loose valve core. The customer does this check first:

1. Inflate to 15 PSI.
2. Spray soapy water on the valve. Bubbles mean air escapes there.
3. Turn the valve core a quarter turn clockwise with the valve wrench.
4. Spray again, then check the pressure after 24 hours.

If the board still loses more than 2 PSI in 24 hours, ask for the order number, board size, and days since arrival.

## Problems and what happens next

A colleague handles every shipment. Never promise one in a reply.

- Missing accessory: ship the part. No return needed.
- Slow leak: run the valve check first.
- Still leaking after the valve check, within 30 days of delivery: send a new board.
- Still leaking after the valve check, more than 30 days after delivery: send a patch kit.
- Split seam: always goes to a colleague.

To arrange anything, collect the order number, board size, and a short description of the problem.
